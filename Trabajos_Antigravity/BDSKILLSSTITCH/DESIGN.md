# Design System: MetaAnalysis Dashboard (Academic Publication Standard)
**Project ID:** 14877947718462505040

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The dashboard embodies a strictly "Academic & Scientific Publication" atmosphere. It draws inspiration from Cochrane Reviews, PRISMA guidelines, and top-tier medical journals. The goal is uncompromising clarity, high contrast, and solemnity. It distances itself from "trendy SaaS" looks, favoring a stark, clean, and highly readable light mode optimized for complex data tables and dense systematic review metrics.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Paper White (Base/Primary)** (#ffffff) - Used for all major content areas and backgrounds to simulate physical paper and ensure standard contrast.
* **Neutral Gray (Canvas)** (#f0f2f5) - A very subtle background to separate distinct panels without overshadowing the white cards.
* **Cochrane Navy (Accent)** (#004b87) - The primary brand and action color. It is serious, trustworthy, and heavily associated with medical research and evidence-based medicine.
* **Charcoal Ink (Text Primary)** (#0f172a) - Almost black, strictly for high legibility of data, estimates, and confidence intervals.
* **Graphite (Text Secondary)** (#334155) - For secondary information and methodological notes.

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Font Family:** Inter (Primary interface for neutral, distraction-free reading), JetBrains Mono (Data tables, values, and statistical outputs).
* **Weights:** Regular (400) for body text and data limits. Medium (500) for standard headers. 
* **Character:** Traditional, dense, zero frills.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Panels/Cards:** Shape: Clean, slightly rounded (6px). Background: Pure white. Border: Crisp, thin gray (#e2e8f0). Shadow: Extremely subtle, barely perceptible.
* **Buttons:** Primary buttons use Cochrane Navy with white text. They are flat, eschewing glows or heavy gradients, to keep the focus on the actual statistical outputs.
* **Data Tables:** High contrast, unmistakable borders, utilizing alternating very faint grays if necessary, but primarily relying on structural whitespace and thin borders.

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Structure:** Maximizing data-to-ink ratio. Whitespace is generous but completely purposeful. 
* **Focus:** Charts (Forest, Funnel) and data tables command the primary visual weight. UI elements are subordinate to the data.
