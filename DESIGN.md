# Design System: Metaanalista
**Project ID:** 3845354265794435456

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
- **Vibe:** Professional, Scientific, High-Precision, Minimalist.
- **Atmosphere:** Clean and airy with generous white space to reduce cognitive load during complex research tasks.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Base White (#FDFDFD):** Background and clean workspace areas.
- **Navy Night (#0F172A):** Sidebar, primary typography, and structural accents.
- **Accented Blue (#2E5BFF):** Primary CTA buttons, active states, and focus indicators.
- **Success Emerald (#10B981):** Included papers, verified citations, and positive outcomes.
- **Warning Amber (#F59E0B):** Uncertain papers or items requiring manual review.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Sans-Serif (Inter/Roboto):** Primary font family for maximum readability.
- **Hierarchical Scaling:** Clear distinction between headers (Bold Navy) and body text (Regular Navy/Gray).

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Cards:** White background, subtle shadows (elevation 1-2), and rounded corners (12px).
- **Interactive Pills:** Rounded containers for domain-specific context (Medical, Pharma, Public Health).
- **Navigation:** Vertical sidebar with icon-text pairs and distinct active state.

## 5. Layout Principles
- **Sidebar-Centric:** Persistent navigation on the left (20-25% width).
- **Focused Workspace:** Central area for interactive tasks (Prompting, Screening, Results).
- **Modular Sections:** Grouping related controls (PICO, Effort, Tones) into logical blocks.
